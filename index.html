Apps

iOS apps I've built.

TDLidar Stream LiDAR point cloud data from iPhone to TouchDesigner via OSC for real-time 3D scanning and interactive installations.
ZPTH Turn your iPhone's depth camera into a living visual instrument. 20 real-time depth modes — contour, plasma, chrome, living cells, pixel dither. Capture photo or video, 100% on-device.
Vision Emoji Real-time object detection that overlays matching Apple emojis onto your camera feed. 80+ categories, 1000+ classifications, 100% on-device.
Splat Frame 3D parallax display using TrueDepth face tracking. Move your head to peer through a portal into a virtual room with your photos and videos.

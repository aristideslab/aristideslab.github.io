Four apps, all on-device.

TDLidar

Stream LiDAR point cloud data from iPhone to TouchDesigner over OSC for real-time 3D scanning and interactive installations.

ZPTH

Turn your iPhone's depth camera into a living visual instrument. 20 real-time depth modes — contour, plasma, chrome, living cells, pixel dither. Photo or video, 100% on-device.

Vision Emoji

Real-time object detection that overlays matching Apple emoji onto your camera feed. 80+ categories, 1000+ classifications, 100% on-device.

Splat Frame

3D parallax display using TrueDepth face tracking. Move your head to peer through a portal into a virtual room of your photos and videos.
